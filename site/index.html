What does Ordexa Fulfillment cover?

Receiving and storing your stock, order confirmation by phone, pick and pack, last-mile delivery, cash collection and settlement reporting.

Tunisia, Algeria, Morocco and Egypt, with regional hubs and a connected carrier network.

How do I connect my store?

Create an account, then connect your e-commerce platform or import your orders. Our team guides you through onboarding.

What are the fees?

Pricing depends on volume, products and destination. Send us a request and we will share a clear quote.

How and when do I get paid?

Delivered COD orders are reconciled and settled on a regular schedule, with a detailed statement for each payout.
