From signup to payout in six simple steps

Launch quickly and let our team operate the logistics while you focus on selling.

Free Sign Up

  1. Create your account

    Sign up for free and tell us about your products, volumes and target markets.

  2. Connect your store

    Link your e-commerce platform, import orders by file, or use our API.

  3. Send your stock

    Ship your inventory to our hub. We receive, count and shelve it, then update your dashboard.

  4. We confirm every order

    Our call center verifies each order with the customer before it goes to the warehouse.

  5. We pick, pack and ship

    Orders are picked, scan-checked, packed and handed to the best carrier.

  6. Delivery and payout

    The parcel is delivered and cash collected. You receive a clear settlement statement.
